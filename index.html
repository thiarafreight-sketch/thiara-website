Our Services

Comprehensive freight transportation and logistics solutions for carriers, brokers, and shippers

Full-Service Freight Logistics

What Thiara Enterprises Delivers

From initial dispatch to final delivery confirmation, we handle the details so your freight moves smoothly.

🚛

Freight Transportation

Thiara Enterprises provides professional freight transportation services for dry van and reefer loads across regional and interstate routes. We coordinate with drivers and carriers to ensure safe, timely, and compliant delivery of goods.

  • Dry van freight transportation
  • Reefer / temperature-controlled freight
  • Regional and long-haul routes
  • FMCSA and DOT compliant operations
📡

Dispatch Coordination

Our dispatch team manages load assignments, communicates route details, handles check-ins, and keeps operations running efficiently from first mile to last. We act as the critical link between carriers and brokers.

  • Load booking and assignment
  • Driver check-in and monitoring
  • Route guidance and optimization
  • Broker and shipper coordination
📞

Carrier Communication

Professional, timely, and accurate communication between carriers, brokers, and shippers is essential to freight logistics. We deliver structured carrier communications that meet industry standards and broker expectations.

  • Scheduled check calls
  • ETA reporting and confirmations
  • Incident and delay notifications
  • Broker and shipper correspondence
🔄

Load Updates

We provide continuous, real-time load status updates from pickup to delivery — so brokers, shippers, and receivers always know exactly where their freight stands at every stage of transit.

  • Pickup confirmations
  • In-transit status updates
  • Delivery notifications
  • POD (Proof of Delivery) follow-up
📦

Delivery Coordination

We manage delivery appointments, receiver communication, and dock scheduling to ensure smooth freight handoffs and minimize wait times for drivers and facilities alike.

  • Delivery appointment scheduling
  • Receiver and dock communication
  • Last-mile coordination
  • Post-delivery confirmation
🛎️

Customer Support

Our customer support team is available to respond to freight inquiries, address concerns, and provide prompt follow-up — making sure every partner and client receives professional, courteous service.

  • Freight inquiry responses
  • Issue resolution and escalation
  • Document and paperwork support
  • Ongoing client communication
